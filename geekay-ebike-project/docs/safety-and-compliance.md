# Safety and Compliance

This file is a planning checklist, not a substitute for the manufacturer's instructions or professional inspection.

## Battery and charging

- [ ] Battery and charger are the exact compatible products approved by the supplier
- [ ] Battery case and cable insulation have no visible damage
- [ ] Battery is securely mounted and protected from impact and water exposure as specified by the manufacturer
- [ ] Charging location and procedure follow the manufacturer's instructions
- [ ] No improvised battery pack, charger, connector, or protection circuit

## Bicycle inspection

- [ ] Wheel, axle retention, spokes, and tyre inspected by a technician
- [ ] Front and rear brakes inspected and adjusted
- [ ] Brake motor-cutoff function verified by a technician
- [ ] Steering, pedals, chain, and drivetrain operate normally
- [ ] Cables cannot contact tyres, spokes, brakes, or steering joints
- [ ] Lights and reflectors are present and working
- [ ] Fasteners and mounts checked to manufacturer specifications

## Before public-road use

- [ ] Qualified technician has inspected the completed bicycle
- [ ] Adult has confirmed local legal requirements and whether the converted bicycle is road-legal
- [ ] Appropriate helmet and protective equipment are available
- [ ] First check is conducted in a controlled, safe area under adult supervision

If the battery becomes unusually hot, swells, leaks, smells unusual, or is damaged, stop using it, keep people away, and get help from a responsible adult and the supplier or emergency services as appropriate.
