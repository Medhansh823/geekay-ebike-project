The product page describes the kit as a **36 V, 250 W BLDC hub-motor kit** and lists items such as a controller, brake levers with motor cut-off, throttle, light/horn, pedal-assist sensor, wiring, and spokes. The linked variant is labelled **“With 7.8AH Li-ion Battery & 2AMP Charger”** on the product page. Product details and prices can change; treat the seller page and written confirmation as the source of truth.

## Project stages

1. **Research:** confirm the bicycle's wheel size, frame/fork compatibility, brake type, axle fit, and kit variant with the seller or a qualified technician.
2. **Plan:** complete the compatibility checklist and budget before purchasing parts.
3. **Professional installation/check:** have a qualified technician assess mechanical fitment, wiring, battery mounting, braking, and weather protection.
4. **Inspection and testing:** do not ride until the bicycle has passed a stationary inspection and a controlled, supervised check by a qualified adult/technician.
5. **Document:** record confirmed specifications, issues, maintenance, and changes.

## Safety notes

- Do not modify, open, puncture, short, or improvise repairs to a lithium-ion battery or charger.
- Use only the compatible battery and charger specified by the supplier.
- Keep wiring secured away from wheels, brakes, steering, and moving parts.
- Confirm the brakes, wheel retention, tyres, lights, and battery mount are suitable before riding.
- Follow local rules for electric bicycles, including any power, speed, age, helmet, and road-use requirements.
- Ask a qualified adult and e-bike technician to supervise installation and any riding tests.
